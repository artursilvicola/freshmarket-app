## What is the Retail Chains Hub

This is the **core B2B Meetings Zone** of Fresh Market. Retail chains have their stations here, and Business or Premium participants come in for pre-scheduled 15-minute meetings throughout the day. Unlike conventional trade shows you do not chase buyers between booths — the buyer comes to your table at the scheduled time.

Retail chains are the most demanding but most rewarding type of buyer because of:

- **Stable and long-term cooperation** once a contract is signed
- **Great commercial volumes** that scale across hundreds of stores
- **Significant brand awareness** for the supplier when listed in major chains

You can meet 20+ chains in a single day at Fresh Market.

## Confirmed chains 2026

The current line-up is maintained on the homepage `retail_chains_2026` data block. Confirmed 2026 chains include Archelan, Auchan, Biedronka (Jeronimo Martins), Carrefour, Dino, and Rohlik.cz — with more being added through summer.

## How meetings work

1. Register for the **Business** or **Premium** package
2. Provide your offer profile (products, seasonality, certifications, volumes, photos)
3. Buyers review and accept or decline matches
4. Receive your meeting schedule ~1 week before the event
5. Meet on the event day — 15 minutes per meeting

## Practical tips for suppliers

- Have a single-page offer sheet ready (printed and digital)
- Prepare three product samples that travel well
- Bring price + MOQ + lead-time information into the meeting
- Follow up by email within 48 hours
