## One day, three working blocks, one party

Fresh Market 2026 is built around a structured series of pre-arranged B2B meetings between fresh-produce suppliers and retail-chain buyers, distributors, and importers. The day finishes with the Speed Dating session and the Cocktail Dinner Party with the Fresh Market Award ceremony.

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 08:00–09:00 | Registration / Check-in |
| 09:00–10:00 | Opening + networking start |
| 10:00–13:00 | Meetings with retail chains — block 1 |
| 13:00–14:00 | Lunch and networking |
| 14:00–16:00 / 17:00 | Meetings with retail chains — block 2 |
| After 16:00 | Speed Dating |
| 17:00–20:00 | Cocktail Dinner Party + Fresh Market Award |

## How meetings work

Each meeting slot is pre-scheduled in the participant panel. Suppliers submit their offer profile (products, seasonality, certifications, volumes, photos) before the event; buyers review and accept matches. On the event day you arrive at your assigned table at the scheduled time — no chasing, no waiting.

## Why one day is enough

Most participants close 8–14 buyer meetings in a single day. That is more than at typical fresh-produce trade shows where buyers move freely and you may or may not catch them. At Fresh Market, the schedule guarantees attention.
