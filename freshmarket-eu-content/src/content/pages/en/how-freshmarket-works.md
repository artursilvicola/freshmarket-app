## What is Fresh Market

Fresh Market is a focused B2B platform for companies active in fresh produce and related categories. The goal is not simply to exhibit — the goal is to open business conversations, verify commercial fit, and accelerate next steps with retail chains, distributors and importers from Central Europe.

## Why it is different

In traditional fairs, suppliers wait at their own stands and hope the right buyer passes by. At Fresh Market we organize **scheduled 1-on-1 meetings** between matched suppliers and buyers — reducing randomness and increasing the chance of speaking to the right person.

## Main business value

The strongest value of Fresh Market is **speed and relevance**. In one day, participants compare retail offers, validate suppliers, refresh their distributor network — work that normally takes weeks of separate trips.

## History

Fresh Market has been organized since **2008**. The 2026 edition is the **20th**.

## Who the event is for

- **Retail chains and buyers** — screen suppliers, compare offers, review seasonal availability
- **Fresh fruit, vegetable and flower suppliers** — core participant group: producers, exporters, packing companies
- **Distributors, importers and traders** — major part of the event, fastest route to PL/CEE market
- **Service providers** — packaging, seeds, machinery, logistics, software
- **International companies entering PL/CEE** — quickest way to understand the market

## Event scale

- **200–220** participating companies
- **~350** participants on site
- **20–27** retail chains (some online)
- **~20** distributors / importers / traders

## Three event areas

- **Retail Chains Hub** — core B2B Meetings Zone
- **Distributors Hub** — most open and flexible area
- **Exhibitors Hub** — visibility zone for brand exposure

## How matchmaking works

1. **Registration & payment** — earlier = higher priority
2. **Profile & matching data** — Business/Premium provide products, seasonality, certs
3. **Buyer review & approval** — buyers confirm which suppliers they want to meet
4. **Schedule preparation** — organizer builds the meeting plan
5. **Meetings on event day** — ~10 minutes each, focused on commercial topics
