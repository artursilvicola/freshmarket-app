## 1. General provisions

These terms govern participation in Fresh Market 2026 ("the Event"), held on 24 September 2026 at MCC Mazurkas Conference Centre, ul. Poznańska 177, 05-850 Ożarów Mazowiecki, Poland. The Event is organised by **KJOW Sp. z o. o.**, ul. Marii 17/25, 05-803 Pruszków, Poland, NIP 118 197 6336 ("the Organiser").

## 2. Definitions

- **Participant** — a natural person delegated by a company that has registered for the Event and paid the participation fee.
- **Supplier** — a Participant offering fresh produce or related products and services to retail chains, distributors, importers, or exporters.
- **Buyer** — a Participant representing a retail chain, distributor, importer, or exporter.
- **Package** — Standard, Business, or Premium, as described on the Registration page.

## 3. Registration and payment

Registration is binding upon submission of the registration form and acceptance of these terms. The Organiser issues a proforma invoice; payment of the proforma is required at least 14 days before the Event.

Cancellation policy: free of charge until 14 days before the Event; 50% fee between 14 and 7 days before; full fee less than 7 days before. Substitution of the registered Participant by another person from the same company is allowed free of charge until the Event day.

## 4. Rights and obligations of Participants

Participants are obliged to attend the meetings scheduled in the participant panel. Late cancellation of confirmed meetings (less than 48h before) without justification may result in restrictions on participation in future editions. Participants are responsible for the accuracy of data they submit.

The Organiser provides the venue, schedule, matchmaking, catering, and logistics support as described in the package. The Organiser does not guarantee any specific number of meetings or commercial outcome, and is not liable for damages exceeding the participation fee paid.

Data may be shared with other Participants for matchmaking purposes (within the panel only). The detailed Privacy Policy is available on request at freshmarket@freshmarket.eu.
