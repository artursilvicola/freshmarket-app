## Who comes to Fresh Market

Since 2008, Fresh Market has hosted suppliers and buyers from across Europe and beyond. The 2025 edition gathered representatives of 20+ retail chains and over 600 companies in total — international groups like Greenyard, SPAR, Auchan, Biedronka, Carrefour, BILLA, regional chains like Stokrotka, Topaz, Dino, Albert, Profi, ATB, VARUS, Rohlik, Polo Market, Nasz Sklep, IKI, Bingo, Sinclair, Intermarche, Archelan, plus specialist distributors and producers from Spain, Italy, Greece, Türkiye, the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania, Hungary, Ukraine, Lithuania.

The full 2025 line-up with logos is on the homepage (`participants_2025` data block).

## What this means for new participants

When you register for 2026 you join an event with proven density of decision-makers. Buyers come because they closed contracts here last year. Suppliers come because the buyers came. The matchmaking model has been refined over 18 editions.
