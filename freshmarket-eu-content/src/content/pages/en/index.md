## Meet the Market — Fresh Market

### B2B MEETING & TRADE SHOW

Fresh Market is Poland's leading B2B event for the fresh fruit, vegetable, and flower sector — held continuously since 2008.

This is not a typical trade fair. Fresh Market is built around a structured series of business meetings between suppliers and retail chain buyers, distributors, importers, and exporters from Central Europe.

Join and see how one day can open the door to your next business breakthrough.

## About Fresh Market 2026

Fresh Market B2B Meeting and Trade Show is the most important event in the fruit, vegetable, and flower sector in Poland and Central Europe.

Each year more than 200 suppliers of vegetables, fruits, and flowers in one day and in one place take part in arranged B2B meetings with retail chain buyers, distributors, and importers.

Participants are also invited to online meetings with retail chains organized throughout the year.

The event is also popular amongst manufacturers of products for agribusiness, packaging for the industry, seeds, machinery and related services.
