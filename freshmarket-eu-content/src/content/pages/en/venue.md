## Address

**MCC Mazurkas Conference Centre**
ul. Poznańska 177, 05-850 Ożarów Mazowiecki, Poland

The venue sits ~20 km west of central Warsaw, with dedicated conference halls, free participant parking, and an on-site hotel — the same venue used by Fresh Market for several past editions.

## How to get there

- **From Warsaw Chopin Airport (WAW):** ~25 km, 30 min by car
- **From Warsaw Central Station:** ~20 km, train to Ożarów Mazowiecki + taxi (~10 min) or direct taxi from station (~25 min)
- **By car:** free participant parking on-site, signed from the S8 expressway
- **Shuttle bus:** Fresh Market arranges a shuttle from central Warsaw on the morning of the event — schedule shared with registered participants ~7 days before

## Floor plan

The conference centre is laid out across one main hall divided into the **Retail Chains Hub**, the **Distributors Hub**, the **Speed Dating** zone, and the **catering / networking** area. The detailed plan with table numbers is shared with all participants in the panel approximately 7 days before the event.

## Hotel options

- **Mazurkas Conference Centre & Hotel** (on-site) — special rates for Fresh Market participants, booking link in the confirmation email
- **Hotel Mercure Warszawa Centrum** (central Warsaw) — for those preferring to stay downtown
- **Hotel Hilton Warsaw City** (Wola district) — close to public transport, taxi to venue ~30 min

A consolidated booking guide with rates is shared with participants ~6 weeks before the event.
