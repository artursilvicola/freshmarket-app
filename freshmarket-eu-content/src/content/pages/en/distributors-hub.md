## What is the Distributors Hub

The most **open and flexible area** of Fresh Market. Distributors, importers and traders are available throughout the day for unscheduled, walk-in meetings — no advance booking required.

For many international companies this is the **fastest route to the Polish and CEE market**: a single distributor can place a new SKU in dozens of regional accounts within weeks, without you running a chain-by-chain commercial programme.

## Why distributors matter

Cooperation with distribution companies is the perfect choice for **smaller suppliers** entering new markets, when the volumes you can commit to are not yet at full chain scale. Distributors absorb logistics complexity, stock risk, and the local commercial relationship — letting you focus on production. They are also the right partner if you want to **understand the specifics of a market step by step** before you sign with a chain directly.
