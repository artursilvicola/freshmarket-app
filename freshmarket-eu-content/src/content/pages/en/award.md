## About the Award

Fresh Market Award is the industry recognition for innovation in fresh produce — packaging, varieties, sustainability, retail-ready solutions and services.

## Categories

- New variety / new product
- Best packaging innovation
- Sustainability award
- Best retail-ready solution
- Service / technology of the year

## Important dates

- **1 June 2026** — Submissions open
- **17 August 2026** — Submissions deadline
- **24 September 2026** — Awards ceremony at the event
