§ 6. PreConnect — sending product submissions

  1. The Supplier may create Submissions in the Service panel and send them to selected Retailers.
  2. Each Submission sent to a Retailer consumes 1 credit from the Supplier's active Package.
  3. The "14-day rule": if the Buyer does not open the Submission within 14 days of the send date, the credit is automatically returned to the Supplier's account.
  4. Opening the Submission by the Buyer results in final settlement of the credit (seen-billing model).
  5. Every Submission undergoes Admin moderation before being sent to the Buyer. The Operator reserves the right to reject a Submission, in particular if:
    • the content is unlawful, contrary to good practice, or unfair;
    • the Submission contains misleading information;
    • the product category does not match the selected Retailer's profile.
  6. Submissions are dispatched on the first Tuesday of each month — all Submissions approved in a given month reach the Retailers in a single batch mailing.
  7. The Supplier may at any time delete their own Submission — provided it has not yet been sent to any Retailer. After the first send, deletion is not possible (the Submission lives in the Operator's process pipeline).

§ 7. Fresh Market 2026 B2B Meetings

  1. The FM B2B Meetings module is available to Suppliers on the Premium Package during the Fresh Market 2026 event.
  2. Schedule of operational phases:
    • 1–16 September 2026 — selection of Retailers by the Supplier; acceptance of Suppliers by Buyers.
    • 17–22 September 2026 — pairing algorithm + manual Admin corrections.
    • 22 September 2026 — publication of the final meeting schedule.
    • 24 September 2026 — Fresh Market 2026 — B2B Meetings day.
  3. The Supplier may indicate a maximum of 5 priority Retailers (marked ⭐) and any number of backup Retailers (marked 👍).
  4. The pairing algorithm prioritises meetings according to the following hierarchy:
    1. Mutual confirmation (Supplier selected the Retailer + Retailer accepted the Supplier).
    2. Package purchase date (earlier payment = earlier slot in the schedule).
    3. Priority Retailers (⭐) before backup Retailers (👍).
  5. Each Supplier receives meeting numbers determining the call order on the day of the event. The Operator does not guarantee a specific meeting time — calls are run in queue mode via the event screen and the application.
  6. The Operator does not guarantee a minimum number of meetings for any Supplier — the number of meetings depends on Retailers' decisions to accept the offer.
  7. A refund of the Premium Package fee in case of no meetings is not available, in line with § 12.
