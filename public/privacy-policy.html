4. With whom we share data

⚠️ KEY PRINCIPLE: The Operator does not sell, rent, or transfer User data to external parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only within agreed commercial cooperation — and only with those parties to whom the User has chosen to send a Submission or with whom the User has chosen to meet.

4.1 Mutual Supplier ↔ Buyer visibility — asymmetric

Visibility is deliberately asymmetric — we protect Buyers' personal data (people representing Retailers / distributors). The Supplier receives information about the Retailer as an organisation, not about a specific Buyer as a natural person.

What the Buyer sees about the Supplier

The Buyer sees the Supplier's full data (company name, logo, description of activity, Submission content with prices and photos, contact-person details — first name, last name, business email, phone) ONLY when:

  • the Supplier sent a Submission to the Retailer represented by the Buyer (PreConnect module), or
  • the Supplier and the Buyer were matched for an FM B2B Meeting (after mutual confirmation).

The Supplier's full contact data is deliberately exposed to the Buyer — the Supplier knowingly and voluntarily shares their data with the selected Retailer, since their goal is to enter into commercial cooperation.

What the Supplier sees about the Buyer / Retailer

The Supplier DOES NOT see the Buyer's personal data (first name, last name, email, or phone of a specific person representing the Retailer). The Supplier sees only the Retailer data as an organisation:

  • name of the Retailer / distributor (e.g. "Biedronka", "Lidl Polska", "Carrefour"),
  • buying categories of the Retailer (e.g. "fruits", "vegetables", "flowers"),
  • country of the Retailer, Retailer logo (if shared by the Retailer).

The Submission status visible to the Supplier is: "sent to Biedronka", "opened by Retailer", "expired" — without identifying the specific person who opened it.

Communication after mutual contact

If the Buyer decides to respond to a received Submission (via an email from the Retailer's domain, by phone, or in person during FM B2B Meetings), then from that moment on both parties have exchanged contacts and may continue cooperation outside the Service.

The Operator does not actively pass Buyer data to the Supplier — it is the Buyer who decides whether and how to disclose their personal data to the Supplier.

6. Data security

The Operator applies the following technical and organisational measures:

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic between the browser and the Service runs over HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • Password encryption — User passwords are stored only as hashes (bcrypt).
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) — database-level security rules ensure that the Supplier sees only their own data and the Buyer sees only data of the Retailer to which they belong.
  • Storage with signed URLs — files (logos, product photos) are protected by authorisation tokens.
  • Backups — Supabase runs daily database backups with 7–30 days retention (depending on plan).
  • Security logs — all administrative operations are logged.
  • Limited administrative access — access to the admin panel is restricted to designated KJOW Sp. z o.o. staff.
  • Updates — the Service and its programming dependencies are regularly updated to the latest versions.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses cookies that are necessary for sign-in and user session to work. Without these cookies, using the Service is not possible. They do not require consent (they are exempt from the consent requirement under the GDPR / ePrivacy).

Examples: sb-access-token, sb-refresh-token (Supabase Auth)

7.2 Statistical cookies (optional)

The Service does not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or Hotjar. We do not collect user-behaviour data for marketing purposes.

7.3 LocalStorage

The application uses the browser's localStorage to:

  • store the session token (sign-in)
  • remember progress in multi-step forms (before submission)
  • remember selections in the testing checklist (if you use it)

localStorage data is stored only locally in the User's browser — it is not transmitted to the Operator.

8. Profiling and automated decisions

8.1 FM B2B Meetings pairing algorithm

The Operator uses a pairing algorithm between Suppliers and Retailers for the FM B2B Meetings module. The algorithm takes into account:

  • mutual selection by Supplier and Retailer,
  • Package purchase date,
  • Package type (Premium / Standard),
  • the Buyer's buying-category preferences.

This is not an "automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR" producing legal effects or significantly affecting an individual. The algorithm serves only to order meetings during the event. The Admin retains the full right to manually adjust the schedule.

8.2 No marketing profiling

The Operator does not profile Users for advertising purposes, marketing segmentation, or Retailer-side commercial decisions. Commercial decisions are made by Buyers themselves based on the content of Submissions.
